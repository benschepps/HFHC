Strategy

Why a gradual ramp

What we want to avoid is flooding the feed before we've found our voice. It's vital we establish the vibe we're going for before anything else happens. We don't want to bombard potential customers with directionless posts at a volume they can't keep up with.

The first 15 days are purely internal. No posts, just building. By the time anything goes live every asset is reviewed, every caption is written, and the accounts already feel like they belong to something worth following. We only get one first impression.

Staff as the content engine

The fieldhouse is a community entity at its core. Youth sports, family time at the entertainment centers, all of it lives and breathes through the people who work there. The more our staff are in the thick of the content, the less corporate the whole thing feels.

It's a lot to ask of them, and we want to be thoughtful about how we bring them into it. But a facility as advanced as this one deserves to have its staff be the face of it. When the people who actually work there are the ones showing it off, audiences feel that. That's the kind of content that builds a real community around a place.

Sports and entertainment working together

It's important for the sports and entertainment sectors to work together. Like the offense and defense in sports. We want the same customers leaving the courts or turf to go straight to the restaurants and bars. The feed needs to reflect that relationship. Not two separate venues sharing an Instagram account, but one place where the whole experience lives.

Tone of voice

High energy but never hollow. Think Dude Perfect. Exciting, a little chaotic, but equally business savvy and fan engaging. We want people watching our content to feel like they're already part of something. The staff are excited, the facility is incredible, and that should come through naturally. Not because we told them to be. Because they genuinely are.

Content pillars
Sports & courts

Family entertainment

Pre-launch Days 1–15 Internal only · nothing posted

Production phase. Build the asset library, brief the staff, shoot everything. Nothing goes live until Phase 2.

Day 1–2
strategy
Content brief & brand alignment
Brainstorm session style. Lock tone of voice, Nevada palette, caption style, hashtag set. Align with leadership before a single frame is shot.
Day 2–3
production
Staff briefings, storyboard & talent prep
Brainstorm session style. Meet each section lead. Storyboard the relay shoot — sequence, flow, transitions. Brief on-camera talent on expectations. Keep it casual, authentic energy not performance.
Day 3–4
production
Hero video shoot — staff relay tour
Film each section with its lead. Two cuts planned: polished for IG, looser for TikTok. Capture B-roll throughout.
Day 5–6
production
Staff spotlight shoots
Film each section lead in their space. Basketball, volleyball, turf, entertainment. These become the Staff spotlight posts — staff are the face of each one. Candid format, conversational tone.
Day 7–8
production
Facility photography day
Still photography across all sections. Courts, turf, bowling, laser tag, restaurant. Nevada palette styling.
Day 9–11
production
Entertainment B-roll & preview shoots
Multi-day shoot covering both entertainment preview posts. Part 1 — bowling, laser tag, arcade. Part 2 — miniature golf, birthday rooms, restaurant and bar. All footage captured and ready to edit before Phase 2 starts.
Day 11–12
editing
Hero video edit — both cuts
Cut the IG reel and TikTok version from relay footage. Music licensed, captions drafted, both versions exported.
Day 12–13
creative
Graphic asset production
Lock branded templates — logo placement, lower thirds, sport feature frames, color overlays, and Henderson by the numbers stat graphic. Every post should feel like it came from the same place.
Day 13–14
copy
Caption & copy bank
Write captions for all Phase 2 posts in advance. Hashtag sets per category. Tone check against brand brief.
Day 14
review
Internal content review
Full asset review with leadership. Approve hero video, graphics, copy. Flag anything needing a reshoot or revision.
Day 15
launch prep
Accounts set up & scheduled
Profiles optimized — bio, link, profile photo, highlights covers. Phase 2 Day 16 post scheduled and ready to fire.
